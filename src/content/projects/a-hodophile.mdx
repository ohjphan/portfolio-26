A hodophile is someone who loves to travel. I’ve always been one — curious about people, culture, landmarks, and the histories that shape a place.

This project was a way to keep that curiosity in motion while I was deep in product design work. I gathered fifteen favorite travel quotes and paired each with photographs from my own trips — Vietnam, Italy, Taiwan, New York, Australia, Spain, and more — then built a small front-end site to share them.

It was equal parts photography practice and code practice: composing a frame, sequencing a story, and learning enough HTML and CSS to give the work a public home.
