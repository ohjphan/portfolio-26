How it works

Five steps. You leave with a banner file you can print yourself.

  1. 1

    Your team

    Add the team name, choose boys or girls, and pick the color. A second color is there if you want it.

  2. 2

    Your vibe

    Choose up to three moods. Add a note if you want something in the design, or left out.

  3. 3

    Three concepts

    Copy the brief into ChatGPT. You get three different banners to compare.

  4. 4

    Finalize it

    Pick a favorite. One more prompt gets it ready for a 5 × 3 ft banner.

  5. 5

    Print it

    You print it yourself. A banner and a stand are waiting when you are ready.
